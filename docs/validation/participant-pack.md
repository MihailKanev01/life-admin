# Participant Validation Pack

## Purpose

Use the clickable prototype to test the product concept with real people before we lock MVP scope.

## Session length

Target: 15–20 minutes.

## Moderator rule

Do not explain the structure before the participant tries it.

Say:

> "This is a prototype. There are no right answers. Please think aloud and do what you naturally would do."

## Task sequence

### 1. First impression
Open Home.

Ask:
"What do you think this page is for?"

Do not explain.

### 2. Find today's priority
"Tell me what needs your attention today."

Observe:
- first item selected;
- whether they understand status;
- whether the hierarchy feels obvious.

### 3. Add something
"Imagine your car insurance expires on June 14. Show me how you would save that."

Observe:
- whether they choose Quick Add;
- whether the language feels natural;
- whether AI proposal is trusted.

### 4. Find the car
"Show me where you would keep everything related to your car."

Observe:
- whether Things makes sense;
- whether object-first organization is intuitive.

### 5. Find a document
"Where would you look for your car insurance document?"

Observe:
- whether contextual documents make sense;
- whether a separate Documents top-level section is missed.

### 6. Handle a reminder
"You cannot deal with the reminder today. Show me what you would do."

Observe:
- Done vs Snooze;
- whether rescheduling is obvious.

### 7. Check payments
"Show me what recurring payments you have coming up."

Observe:
- whether Payments scope is clear;
- whether summary metrics help or distract.

### 8. Search
"Find anything related to your car."

Observe:
- whether global search is discoverable;
- whether users expect search to span all types.

## Post-task questions

1. What was the most useful part?
2. What felt unnecessary?
3. What did you expect to be somewhere else?
4. What would make you stop using this?
5. What do you currently use instead?
6. Which problem would you expect this to save you from?
7. What information would you be uncomfortable storing here?
8. What would you want this product to do automatically?

## Evidence capture

For each task record:
- first action;
- hesitation;
- wrong path;
- time to completion;
- whether help was needed;
- exact phrase used;
- severity of friction (1–5).

## Decision rule

Do not turn a feature request into a feature automatically.

A repeated workflow across participants is stronger evidence than a single opinion.

## Output

After 10–15 sessions produce:
- participant synthesis;
- recurring pain clusters;
- revised persona;
- revised JTBD;
- revised feature priority;
- confirmed/rejected IA assumptions;
- final MVP scope proposal.
