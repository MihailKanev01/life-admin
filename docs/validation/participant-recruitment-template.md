# Participant Recruitment Template

Use this only for real participant recruitment. Do not imply that the product is validated.

## Who we're looking for

We're testing a prototype for personal life administration: keeping track of things like car/home admin, recurring bills, reminders, purchases, warranties and documents.

We're looking for adults who regularly manage some combination of:
- household bills;
- vehicle maintenance or insurance;
- purchases, warranties or returns;
- personal documents and deadlines.

No technical background is required.

## Outreach message

Hi! I'm testing an early prototype for a personal life-admin app and I'm looking for a few people to try it.

It takes about 25–35 minutes. I'd ask you to talk through how you currently handle everyday admin and then complete a few simple tasks in the prototype.

There are no right or wrong answers — I'm testing the product, not you. I won't need passwords, bank details or sensitive documents.

Would you be available for a short session?

## Facilitator note

Recruit participants who can describe real recent admin situations. Avoid leading with the product's solution or expected benefits.

Record participant profile and evidence in docs/validation/interview-tracker.md.
