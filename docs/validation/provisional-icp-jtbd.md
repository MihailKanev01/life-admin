# Provisional ICP & JTBD — Online Evidence

> Status: hypothesis only. Derived from secondary online evidence and competitor research.
> 
> This is intentionally not labelled as a validated persona.

## Provisional primary user

**Working adult who owns or manages several real-world responsibilities across home, vehicle, bills/subscriptions and important documents, and currently relies on a mixture of Calendar, Reminders, Notes, spreadsheets, email and paper.**

Typical characteristics:
- has multiple recurring obligations;
- is the person who notices when something needs doing;
- does not want to become a “productivity person”;
- values low maintenance more than deep customization;
- sometimes needs supporting context such as a receipt, warranty, service history or payment detail.

## Core JTBD hypothesis

> **When personal admin starts spreading across tools and deadlines, help me know what matters now and give me the context needed to act, without making me maintain another complicated system.**

## Related jobs

### Job 1 — Remember
“When does this need attention?”

### Job 2 — Understand
“What exactly is this about, and what information belongs with it?”

### Job 3 — Act
“What do I need before I can actually finish it?”

### Job 4 — Reconstruct
“When did I last do this, and what happened?”

### Job 5 — Hand off
“Who is responsible for this, and are we waiting on someone else?”

## Likely high-value scenarios

| Scenario | Why it matters | Current workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle insurance / service | deadline + recurring cycle + documents + history | reminder + notes/photos + papers |
| Home maintenance | recurring + unknown obligations + vendor/history context | calendar + spreadsheet + email |
| Subscription renewal | payment + due date + cancellation/renewal context | calendar/reminder + banking app |
| Warranty / purchase | expiry + proof + purchase details | email + files + reminder |
| Household handoff | one person currently carries the mental load | shared reminders/messages/calendar |

## Anti-personas / weak-fit users

### Productivity power user
Already happy with Todoist/Notion and enjoys maintaining structured systems.

### Pure file-storage user
Main need is archival storage, not action.

### Banking-first user
Wants transactions, budgeting, account aggregation or payment execution.

### Property professional
Needs multi-property/tenant/project workflows beyond Life Admin's intended scope.

## Competitive implication

The product should not try to replace every workflow.

The job is strongest where the existing options leave a gap between:
**“I can store a reminder”** and **“I know enough to confidently act.”**

That gap is the core thing we must test.

## Research priority

The first real participants should deliberately include:
- people who currently use only Calendar/Reminders;
- people who combine multiple tools/spreadsheets;
- people responsible for a car/home or other maintained assets.

We need to discover whether the same “context gap” appears across these profiles or only in a narrow subset.

## Decision rule

Do not lock this ICP or JTBD from online evidence.

After direct sessions, compare:
- observed workflow;
- current workaround;
- frequency;
- consequences of forgetting;
- emotional cost;
- willingness to switch;
- repeatability across participants.

Then replace this hypothesis with evidence-backed ICP/JTBD.
