# Phase 1 — Recruitment Kit

## Target participants

Prioritize adults who manage several recurring real-life obligations and currently use a mix of apps, notes, calendars, email, spreadsheets, or memory.

Good-fit examples:
- car owner with insurance/service/inspection/admin;
- renter/homeowner managing bills and maintenance;
- person managing multiple subscriptions and purchases;
- person responsible for shared household administration.

Avoid recruiting only developers, product designers, or people who already use a sophisticated personal-management system. We need ordinary behavior, not expert opinions.

## Recruitment message

> Здравей! Правя кратък тест на ново приложение за организиране на важни неща от ежедневието — например документи, плащания, напомняния, поддръжка и покупки. Търся хора за 15–20 минутен разговор, в който ще ти покажа прототип и ще те помоля да изпълниш няколко прости задачи. Няма правилни или грешни отговори — тествам приложението, не теб. Не е нужно да се подготвяш предварително.

## Session rules

- 15–20 minutes.
- Remote screen share or in-person.
- Ask the participant to think aloud.
- Do not coach unless the task becomes blocked.
- Do not reveal the intended information architecture before the task.
- Do not use leading questions.
- Record only consented notes/audio/video.

## Minimum sample

Target: 10–15 participants.

Aim for diversity across:
- age/life stage;
- household responsibility;
- number of recurring obligations;
- comfort with apps;
- current organizational strategy.

## Evidence quality

High-quality evidence:
- observed behavior;
- repeated workflow;
- repeated problem;
- concrete existing workaround;
- willingness to change current behavior.

Lower-quality evidence:
- hypothetical feature wishes;
- vague "this is nice";
- single-person edge cases;
- guesses about what other people need.

## Consent

Before recording audio/video, explicitly ask for permission. Participation should not require disclosure of sensitive personal data. Use fictional example data during prototype tasks.
