# Phase 1 — Synthesis Framework

## Goal

Convert individual interviews into product decisions without treating every feature request as evidence.

## Step 1 — Cluster evidence

Group observations into:
- fragmented admin;
- attention/deadline management;
- capture effort;
- recurring obligations;
- documents;
- payments;
- household coordination;
- trust/privacy;
- navigation/mental model;
- automation/AI.

## Step 2 — Count repeated behavior

For each cluster record:
- participants observed;
- participants reporting the problem;
- current workaround;
- severity;
- frequency;
- consequence of failure.

Do not infer prevalence from fewer than 10 participants.

## Step 3 — Separate problem from solution

Example:

Problem:
"I keep forgetting annual insurance renewals."

Solution request:
"Add a WhatsApp bot."

The problem can validate while the requested solution remains unvalidated.

## Step 4 — Re-score hypotheses

For H1–H10 in `docs/validation/hypotheses.md`, mark:
- Supported
- Mixed
- Not supported
- Insufficient evidence

Add evidence IDs linking to participant records.

## Step 5 — Decide

The Phase 1 synthesis must produce:
- primary target user;
- top 3–5 recurring workflows;
- revised JTBD;
- revised Things mental model;
- revised notification model;
- Payments scope decision;
- document value decision;
- household-sharing decision;
- AI role decision;
- final MVP feature list;
- explicit rejected/deferred features.

## Gate

Phase 1 passes only when:
- 10–15 quality sessions are complete;
- recurring patterns are documented;
- hypotheses are scored with evidence;
- MVP scope has been updated;
- decision log records what changed and why.
